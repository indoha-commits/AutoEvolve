# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must communicate respectfully, review ideas on technical merit, accept constructive feedback, and avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or disclosure of another person's private information.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, community channels, and public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

Maintainers must apply these rules consistently and disclose conflicts of interest when handling a report.
