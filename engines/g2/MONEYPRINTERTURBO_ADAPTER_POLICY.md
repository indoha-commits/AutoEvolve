# MoneyPrinterTurbo Adapter Policy

MoneyPrinterTurbo is an optional, isolated video-render backend and implementation reference, not the G2 controller. Its MIT-licensed patterns can help with FFmpeg assembly, timed captions, media-provider adapters, local assets, and batch execution.

G2 retains authority over the G1 contract, claim IDs, brand rules, approved assets, checksums, review state, and publishing permissions. MoneyPrinterTurbo script generation, autonomous stock selection, bundled music, and social publishing are disabled at this boundary. Any future adapter must accept a finalized `VideoManifest`, return files plus provenance, and have no permission to rewrite copy or publish.

Music must be supplied through a separately licensed library; bundled/example tracks are not assumed safe for commercial use.
